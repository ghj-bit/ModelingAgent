# Focal unresolved structural assumption (ranked #1 by decision impact)

My draft sizes checked-baggage EDS capacity for two hub airports at their busiest hour from the peak-hour departure table. I convert a "screening-fleet requirement" into a procurement recommendation through one structural assumption I have not yet committed to: how far the peak-hour **departure bank** can be re-timed/re-spread across airlines, given that passengers arrive 45–120 minutes before departure, and whether that re-timing is a safe basis for the headline number.

**Focal question:** Which structural assumption about *departure-bank re-timing and stakeholder cooperation* should drive the headline procurement recommendation, and how should confidence be bounded when that cooperation is not enforceable by the airport?

**Tightly coupled sub-questions:** (1) What is the principal failure mode if a smoothed/flattened departure bank cannot be assumed? (2) What applicability boundary or class of external evidence should condition the recommendation?

I have deliberately not stated a preferred answer.
