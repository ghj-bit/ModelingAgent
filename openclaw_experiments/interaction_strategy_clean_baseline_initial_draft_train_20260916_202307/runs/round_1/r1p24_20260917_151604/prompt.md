**2017 ICM Problem E: Sustainable Cities Needed!**

**Background:**
Communities are adopting smart growth initiatives to achieve long-term, sustainable urban planning. Smart growth aims to make cities economically prosperous, socially equitable, and environmentally sustainable. With rapid urbanization, it's projected that by 2050, 66% of the global population will live in urban areas, adding 2.5 billion people to urban populations. Urban planning is crucial to provide equitable and sustainable living conditions.

Smart growth, originating in the 1990s, addresses urban sprawl and farmland loss. Its ten principles are:
1. Mix land uses
2. Utilize compact building design
3. Offer diverse housing options
4. Create walkable neighborhoods
5. Foster distinctive communities with a strong sense of place
6. Preserve open spaces and critical environmental areas
7. Direct development towards existing communities
8. Provide diverse transportation options
9. Ensure predictable, fair, and cost-effective development decisions
10. Encourage community and stakeholder collaboration

These principles must be adapted to each community's unique needs, considering demographics, growth needs, and geographical conditions.

**Tasks:**
The International City Management Group (ICM) seeks assistance in applying smart growth theories to city design globally. Select two mid-sized cities (population 100,000-500,000) on different continents.

1. Define a metric to measure smart growth success, considering the three E’s of sustainability and/or the 10 smart growth principles.
2. Research and evaluate the current growth plans of the selected cities against smart growth principles using your metric.
3. Develop a smart growth plan for both cities for the coming decades, justifying your choices based on geography, growth rates, and economic opportunities. Evaluate the success of your plans using your metric.
4. Rank the initiatives within your smart growth plan from most to least potential using your metric. Compare and contrast the initiatives and their rankings between the two cities.
5. Explain how your plan supports a 50% population increase by 2050.

**References:**
1. Smart Growth America: https://smartgrowthamerica.org/
2. EPA Smart Growth Publication: https://www.epa.gov/smartgrowth/smart-growth-publication
3. UN World Urbanization Prospects: https://esa.un.org/unpd/wup/Publications/Files/WUP2014-Highlights.pdf
4. EPA Smart Growth Guide: http://www.sustainablecitiesinstitute.org/Documents/SCI/Report_Guide/Guide_EPA_SmartGrowthGHGReduction_2011.pdf
5. The Smart Growth Manual by Duany, Speck, and Lydon.
