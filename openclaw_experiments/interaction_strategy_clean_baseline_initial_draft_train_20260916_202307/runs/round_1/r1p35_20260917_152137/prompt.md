**2023 ICM Problem D: Prioritizing the UN Sustainability Goals**

**Background**
The United Nations (UN) has established 17 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) aimed at improving global living conditions. These goals are interconnected, meaning progress in one can affect others positively or negatively. Achieving these goals is complex due to funding limitations, technological advances, global pandemics, climate change, regional conflicts, and refugee movements.

**Task Overview**
1. **Network Creation**: Develop a network illustrating the relationships between the 17 SDGs.
2. **Priority Setting**: Use the network to prioritize goals that can most effectively advance the UN's mission. Evaluate the effectiveness of each priority and project achievable outcomes over the next decade.
3. **Impact of Achievements**: Analyze how achieving one SDG (e.g., no poverty or zero hunger) would alter the network and affect priorities. Consider proposing additional goals to the UN.
4. **Influence of Global Factors**: Discuss how technological advances, pandemics, climate change, wars, and refugee movements impact the network and priorities.
5. **Application to Other Organizations**: Explain how this network approach can assist other entities in setting their goals.
