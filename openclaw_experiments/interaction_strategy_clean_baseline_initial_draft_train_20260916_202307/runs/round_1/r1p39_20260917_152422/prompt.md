**2024 MidMCM Problem C: The Modeling Musical Tour**

**Overview:**
Taylor Swift's Eras Tour, concluding in December, included 152 shows, each lasting about 3.5 hours. In the US, each concert attracted approximately 72,000 fans, generating $13 million per show, making it the first tour to earn over $1 billion. Despite the success, ticket acquisition was challenging due to high demand and prices. Swift is known for her generosity towards her team, offering significant bonuses to crew members.

**Task:**
As a tour manager at MidMCM Music Agency, you are tasked with planning a tour for a musical performer of your choice. Analyze the performer's past tours to develop a model for their next tour.

**Steps to Follow:**

1. **Familiarize with a Performer:**
   - Choose a performer with a past concert tour.
   - Investigate the reasons for their tour, its duration, number of shows, and locations.
   - Identify factors for planning a tour, such as single show logistics, multiple show coordination, and elements of a successful tour.

2. **Develop a Model:**
   - Consider aspects like the number of concerts, venues, and ticket prices.
   - Determine the best plan to achieve goals such as maximizing attendance, venues, profits, or other objectives.

3. **Apply the Model:**
   - Analyze a past tour using your model to identify strengths and weaknesses.
   - Plan a future tour, suggesting improvements and strategies based on your model.

4. **Share Your Model:**
   - Write a one- to two-page letter to the performer, detailing a new tour plan and explaining how it meets their goals.

5. **Reflect:**
   - Consider the model's applicability to other performers and its limitations.

**Submission Requirements:**
- A solution of up to 25 pages, including:
  - One-page Summary Sheet
  - Complete solution
  - One- to two-page letter
