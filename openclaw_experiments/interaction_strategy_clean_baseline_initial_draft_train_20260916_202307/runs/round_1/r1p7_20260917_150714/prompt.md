# Problem

Use the provided problem statement and data directly.

Problem ID: `2003_The_Stunt_Person`
Title: The Stunt Person
Source: MCM 2003

An exciting action scene in a movie is going to be filmed, and you are the stunt coordinator! A stunt person on a motorcycle will jump over an elephant and land in a pile of cardboard boxes to cushion their fall. You need to protect the stunt person, and also use relatively few cardboard boxes (lower cost, not seen by camera, etc.). Your job is to: determine what size boxes to use determine how many boxes to use determine how the boxes will be stacked determine if any modifications to the boxes would help generalize to different combined weights (stunt person & motorcycle) and different jump heights
