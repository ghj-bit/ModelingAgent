# Problem A: The Need for Bees (and not just for honey)

Honeybees are essential for human survival due to their role in pollination and honey production. The decline in honeybee populations, known as Colony Collapse Disorder (CCD), is attributed to factors like viruses, pesticides, predators, habitat destruction, and environmental conditions.

### Key Information:
- **Travel Range**: Honeybees can travel up to 20 km, typically staying within 6 km of their hive.
- **Hive Population**: A typical hive contains 20,000 to 80,000 honeybees.
- **Flower Visits**: A single honeybee can visit approximately 2,000 flowers or more in a day.
- **Lifespan**: Honeybees have a shorter lifespan in summer due to high workload, but may live 4-6 months in autumn and winter.
- **Factors Affecting Lifespan**: Activity level, pollen consumption, and protein abundance.

### Tasks:
1. **Model Development**: Create a model to determine the population of a honeybee colony over time.
2. **Sensitivity Analysis**: Identify which factors (e.g., lifespans, egg laying rates) most impact colony size.
3. **Pollination Prediction**: Estimate the number of hives needed to support pollination of a 20-acre (81,000 square meters) land with crops.
4. **Communication**: Develop a non-technical, one-page blog or infographic summarizing your findings.

### Submission Requirements:
- A solution of no more than 25 pages, including:
  - One-page Summary Sheet
  - Complete Solution
  - One-page Blog or Infographic
