## Key results
- Governing relation: `T ≈ t0 + max((N-1)Δt, N·p/C)`; flow-limited term dominates for dense tall buildings.
- Stair budget: `N ≤ (60X - t0)·C / p`.
- Answers: X=15 → ~56 m / 16 fl / ~2400 occ (4×1.5 m stairs);
  X=30 → ~136 m / 39 fl / ~5850 occ (3 stairs + 4 rescue elevators);
  X=60 → ~287 m / 82 fl / ~12300 occ (3 stairs + 4 rescue elevators + rooftop helicopter).
- Finding: a true skyscraper cannot be fully cleared in 15 min by stairs alone.
