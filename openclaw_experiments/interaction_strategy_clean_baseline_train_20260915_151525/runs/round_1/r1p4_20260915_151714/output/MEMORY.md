## Lessons learned
- For "which wheel" problems the decisive physics is the **yaw-dependence** of wheel
  aerodynamic drag: a disc is only marginally better head-on but dramatically better
  in crosswinds (it can even generate thrust at high yaw).
- Real racing disc and spoked wheels can have almost identical mass (Campagnolo
  Vento 1.193 kg vs HED disc 1.229 kg) — the "solid = heavy" premise is a simplification.
- Always sanity-check a bicycle power model against the classic anchor: ~340 W to
  hold 45 km/h on the flat for an 80 kg system.
